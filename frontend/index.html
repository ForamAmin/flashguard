100 slots. 10,000 requests. Exactly 100 winners.

An overselling-proof claim engine for limited-slot drops.

When a flash sale or a limited ticket drop goes live, thousands of requests can hit the same inventory in the same instant. FlashGuard makes it structurally impossible to hand out more slots than exist — proven under real concurrent load, not just designed to.

100 / 100
successful claims out of 10,000 concurrent attempts against 100 slots — zero overselling

Atomic by design

A single Redis Lua script checks and decrements inventory as one indivisible operation, so no two concurrent requests can ever race each other.

Fully auditable

Every claim attempt — successful or rejected — is written to Postgres, giving a durable record for rejection-rate and throughput reporting.

Multi-tenant from day one

Every organization gets its own API key and its own isolated drops. One tenant can never see or claim against another's inventory.

Built with Spring Boot, PostgreSQL, and Redis. Load tested with k6.
